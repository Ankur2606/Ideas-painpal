# Grill Me: pressure test of the recommendation

1. **You recommend an idea whose novelty search was cut short. Why trust it?** You should not fully. The deep dive found no competitor across ~144 Devpost entries reviewed, and a follow-up hunt found only DisputeDesk, which does post-dispute evidence rather than the pre-ship gate. Three more searches is still thin; repeat the hunt weekly.
2. **If PayPal's sandbox always returns ELIGIBLE, what is left?** A policy table plus an AI layer. That scores Tech 6 and Innovation 5. Wrong Box becomes the better bet.
3. **Isn't "ship to my cousin's address" a niche scam?** The policy clause is general: any redirected or buyer-arranged shipment voids protection. No scam-frequency figure was verified, so Impact is scored 6, not 8.
4. **Your Top 10 includes four ideas whose own deep dives say don't build them. Why list them?** Because their desk scores were high and the reasons they fell are useful. They are marked Tier C.
5. **Is the council independent?** No. Three Claude subagents with different roles. That is correlated evidence.
6. **Why is nothing here an agent-payments protocol play (AP2, ACP, x402)?** PayPal's agent tooling is gated (Store Sync, Agent Ready) and sandbox support is thin. Ideas needing it were penalised on feasibility and risk.
7. **What if the judges are pure PayPal DevRel?** Then #56 and #55 gain ground. That is why #55 is in the Top 3.
8. **Would you submit two entries?** Not recommended in 4.5 weeks; the per-person prize cap is one Grand/HM plus one sponsor prize.
