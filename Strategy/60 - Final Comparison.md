## Reading the comparison
- **The two scores agree on the top 3 and the bottom 3.** They disagree on #56: the judge view rates it above #28 because DevRel judges are the audience; the research score rates #28 higher on impact.
- **Impact is the weakest axis for every finalist (4 to 7).** No finalist has top-tier primary evidence of severity for a named audience. The best-evidenced problems (disputes, holds, late payment) are in [[13 - Problem Evidence Base]], and #23 rests on PayPal policy text, not on a quantified scam frequency. This is the main thing to fix in the pitch.
- **Innovation separates the pack.** Every demotion in [[43 - Score Reconciliation]] was an innovation cut after a close competitor was found.

## Method caveat
The Judge /50 column is one Claude subagent role-playing a judge, not an actual judge. Use it as a lens, not evidence.
