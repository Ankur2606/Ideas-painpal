## Cross-cutting patterns (⚠ inference from the data)
1. **PayPal-native policy and state beats generic AI.** The strongest surviving ideas (#23, #55, Wrong Box) hinge on a PayPal-specific rule or state machine (Seller Protection clause, UNCLAIMED→RETURNED, dispute replacement offer). Generic ideas (spend gates, receipts, shopping agents) lost on innovation.
2. **Deep dives deflated the agent-liability theme (E1) most.** #1, #2 collapsed into Provenant / AgentProof / Amex coverage; only the post-delivery photo check (#3) survived as the spine.
3. **Unhappy paths are an explicit judging signal.** E8 (failure-path intelligence) matches the kickoff advice and gave two of the final ten.
4. **Platforms are moving into storefront-side agent plumbing (E7).** Shopify and Stripe shipped WebMCP tools by default, so #47 was demoted.
5. **Moment-of-send scams (E5) are already served by PayPal itself and by banks,** so the headline idea #32 fell from 89 to 71.
6. **The four example directions from the brief map as:** agentic commerce → E1/E7/E9; small business → E3/E4/E8; intelligent payments → E2/E8/E10; fraud → E3/E5. They were treated as hypotheses, and the winners come from E3 and E8.
