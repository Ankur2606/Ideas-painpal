# C — Landscape: AI Agents × Payments × Commerce × Financial Infrastructure (as of 2026-10-11)

**Purpose:** find unsolved problems and differentiated, PayPal-native opportunities for the PayPal AI Hackathon 2026. Not a hype summary.

**Confidence legend:** ✅ primary source (company/regulator/standards body/paper) · ⚠️ secondary (press, analyst, vendor blog summarizing others) · ❌ unverified (single weak source, or recalled and not re-checked this session) · `⚠ inference` = my analytical conclusion, not a sourced fact.
**Status legend:** **[SHIPPED]** live for real users/merchants · **[PILOT]** limited/closed beta · **[ANNOUNCED]** said, not shown live · **[SPEC]** document/repo exists, adoption unclear · **[RETIRED]**.

Method note: Web research run 2026-10-11 with web search, page fetches and the HN Algolia API. The GitHub API was blocked in this sandbox, so star counts were read from rendered repo pages on the same day and are approximate.

## 0. Ten findings that matter most for idea selection

1. **Checkout-in-chat failed at its first attempt. Discovery-in-chat did not.** OpenAI retired ChatGPT Instant Checkout in March 2026, about six months after launch. Only about 12 Shopify merchants went live, and Walmart saw in-chat conversion about 3× lower than click-out ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/24/openai-revamps-shopping-experience-in-chatgpt-after-instant-checkout.html) ⚠️, [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/what-went-wrong-with-chatgpts-instant-checkout/) ⚠️, [Rye](https://rye.com/blog/openai-chatgpt-checkout-agentic-commerce) ⚠️). Meanwhile Adobe says AI-referred retail traffic rose 393% YoY in Q1 2026 and now converts 42% *better* than other sources ([Decrypt summarizing Adobe](https://decrypt.co/364733/ai-traffic-us-retailers-jumps-q1-agentic-shoppers-outspend-humans?amp=1) ⚠️). → Value is moving to discovery, trust and post-purchase, not "another chat checkout button." `⚠ inference`
2. **No network has a liability rule for "authorized but wrong" agent purchases.** Worldpay's Head of Agentic Commerce wrote in July 2026 that "No liability shift exists yet" and that non-fraud agent disputes (misread instruction, wrong item, out-of-scope) have no clear owner ([Worldpay](https://worldpay.com/insights/articles/agentic-commerce-liability-is-still-being-written) ✅). This is the single biggest unowned problem, and it sits right on top of PayPal's Resolution Center and Purchase Protection.
3. **Protocol sprawl is real, and consolidation is happening through foundations.** AP2 went to FIDO (Apr 2026) ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/agent-payments-protocol-fido-alliance/) ✅). x402 went to the Linux Foundation (Apr 2026) ([LF](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-is-launching-the-x402-foundation-and-welcoming-the-contribution-of-the-x402-protocol) ✅). EMVCo published a draft agentic framework (Sep 2026) ([Digital Transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/emvcos-effort-to-reinforce-trust-in-card-based-agentic-commerce/) ⚠️). Visa now sells one integration spanning TAP, MPP, ACP and UCP ([TechInformed](https://techinformed.com/visa-opens-one-integration-for-ai-agent-payments/) ⚠️). → Building yet another protocol is low-value. Building *translation, evidence and policy layers across protocols* is high-value. `⚠ inference`
4. **Signed mandates do not prove intent if the agent was manipulated before signing.** A systematic AP2 v0.2 analysis catalogued 48 threats, 8 of them High, and concluded exactly that ([Beyond the Mandate, arXiv 2608.23858](https://www.emergentmind.com/papers/2608.23858) ✅). Earlier red-teaming broke a Gemini-based AP2 shopping agent with simple injections ([arXiv 2601.22569](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22569v1) ✅).
5. **Consumers will let agents research. Very few will let them pay.** Only 9% of 25,000+ consumers would let an agent complete a purchase including payment ([Accenture via Diplomat](https://www.thediplomat.ro/2026/06/09/accenture-study-74-of-consumers-are-ready-to-let-an-ai-agent-shop-on-their-behalf/) ⚠️). 46.5% trust no company to manage purchases for them ([Riskified Q1 2026](https://www.riskified.com/press/riskified-agentic-commerce-pulse/) ✅). Trust for small purchases depends on easy returns ([RTB House via Digital Transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/consumers-like-ai-for-product-discovery-but-dont-fully-trust-it-for-purchases/) ⚠️).
6. **Machine micropayments are over-built relative to demand.** By one analysis, x402 volume is about $24M per 30 days on Solana (about 70% share) and has "declined from its 2025 highs" ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-x402-market-share-dominance/) ⚠️). An independent survey of 14,865 x402 Bazaar listings found only 3.5% with organic repeat demand, and the top 10 sellers took 89% of volume ([GitHub survey](https://github.com/rikocr8orh8/x402-bazaar-survey/blob/master/POST.md) ❌ single author).
7. **PayPal is present on the consumer-checkout protocols (ACP, UCP, AP2, Copilot Checkout, Perplexity) but largely absent from the machine-payment layer.** PayPal is not named among the x402 Foundation founding members listed by the Linux Foundation and Coinbase ([LF](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-is-launching-the-x402-foundation-and-welcoming-the-contribution-of-the-x402-protocol) ✅, [Coinbase on X](https://x.com/coinbase/status/2039689438922522728) ⚠️), nor in Mastercard's 30+ Agent Pay for Machines participants ([Mastercard](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2026/june/mastercard-launches-agent-pay-for-machines.html) ✅). `⚠ inference` from absence in the published lists.
8. **PayPal's own management says agentic is a 2028+ revenue story.** Zacks reported in August 2026 that PayPal expects agentic products to "become more meaningful from 2028 onward." Branded checkout grew only 2% in Q2 2026 ([Nasdaq/Zacks](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/paypal-agentic-payments-can-they-reshape-future-commerce) ⚠️). → A hackathon project that defends branded checkout's *trust premium* in agent flows maps directly onto the CEO's problem. `⚠ inference`
9. **Merchant data, not payments, is the bottleneck.** Fewer than 1% of 500 product pages scored 80% on Mirakl's LLM-readiness test ([Beet.TV](https://www.beet.tv/2026/08/fewer-than-1-of-product-pages-are-llm-ready-mirakls-meadon.html) ⚠️). Instant Checkout lacked real-time inventory, coupons, loyalty and store pickup ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/what-went-wrong-with-chatgpts-instant-checkout/) ⚠️).
10. **The legal status of agents on third-party sites is still unsettled.** A district court enjoined Perplexity's Comet from shopping on Amazon under the CFAA in March 2026 ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/03/10/judge-blocks-perplexitys-ai-bot-from-shopping-on-amazon-in-early-test-of-agentic-commerce/) ⚠️). The Ninth Circuit vacated the injunction 3-0 in August 2026, holding that the *user* is the one accessing the site ([Sovereign Magazine](https://www.sovereignmagazine.com/article/ninth-circuit-perplexity-comet-amazon-cfaa-ruling) ⚠️). The case continues.

### 1.2 Protocol-level gaps (what none of them specify)

- **Non-fraud dispute semantics.** None of AP2, ACP, UCP or VI defines what happens when a correctly signed mandate produced the wrong outcome. Mastercard's January 2026 "rules of the road" names intent, credentials and agent identity as goals but assigns no liability ([Mastercard](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/stories/2026/agentic-commerce-rules-of-the-road.html) ✅).
- **Pre-signature context integrity.** Mandates protect data *after* signing. A2A messages and MCP tool outputs that shape the cart *before* signing are outside AP2's protection ([arXiv 2608.23858](https://www.emergentmind.com/papers/2608.23858) ✅).
- **Cross-protocol identity.** Visa and Mastercard each host their own agent-key directory "to start" ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/secure-agentic-commerce/) ✅). A merchant has to check several registries, and there is no wallet-neutral one. `⚠ inference`
- **Consumer instruction fidelity.** Cloudflare raises, and does not resolve, how a merchant should honor specific instructions a consumer gave the agent ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/secure-agentic-commerce/) ✅).
- **Composition cost.** A UCP+AP2 transaction needs about four cryptographic verifications versus one for ACP's SPT ([Eco explainer](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14839408-universal-commerce-protocol-ucp-explained) ⚠️). That burden falls on long-tail merchants outside Shopify.
- **Governance asymmetry.** UCP remains Google-led under Apache-2.0 and has not been moved to a foundation (same source ⚠️).

## 3. Unsolved technical challenges (with evidence)

### 3.1 Liability and chargebacks when an agent buys
- **Fraud is roughly covered. Disputes are not.** With proper agent authentication and token issuance, liability follows existing tokenized-transaction rules: the issuer carries fraud risk and cardholder chargeback rights remain. *Non-fraud* disputes (misread instruction, wrong item, outside authorization) have no clear owner among issuer, acquirer, agent platform and merchant ([Worldpay](https://worldpay.com/insights/articles/agentic-commerce-liability-is-still-being-written) ✅).
- **Merchants' new exposure** (same source): disputes with no signed record of what the shopper authorized, where issuers side with the cardholder. Unverifiable "my agent went rogue" friendly fraud. "Not what I wanted" on non-returnable goods. Bot vs. agent confusion.
- **Existing thresholds don't discount agent disputes.** Visa VAMP "Excessive" at 1.5% (from Apr 2026) and Mastercard ECM tiers count all disputes regardless of initiator ([Chargeflow](https://www.chargeflow.io/blog/agentic-commerce-regulation-what-merchants-need-to-know) ⚠️).
- **Reason codes don't fit.** Agent scenarios "do not fit neatly into existing reason codes" (same ⚠️).
- **US consumer law is silent.** Reg E: $50/$500/unlimited tiers. Reg Z: $50 cap and 60-day billing-error notice. No CFPB guidance on AI agents found ([CFPB Reg E](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1005/) ✅, [Reg Z 1026.12](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/12/) ✅, absence per [Chargeflow](https://www.chargeflow.io/blog/agentic-commerce-regulation-what-merchants-need-to-know) ⚠️). Under the CFPB's Reg E interpretation, a consumer who grants authority to someone who exceeds it is liable unless they revoked it. Whether an agent counts as that "someone" is unresolved (same ⚠️).
- **EU:** PSD2/SCA can block agentic transactions from completing ([Worldpay](https://worldpay.com/insights/articles/agentic-commerce-liability-is-still-being-written) ✅).
- `⚠ inference`: PayPal already adjudicates millions of non-card disputes ("item not as described", "item not received") in its own rulebook. It can define an **agent-dispute taxonomy and evidence standard** inside its own network without waiting for Visa, Mastercard or EMVCo.

### 3.2 Agent authentication and identity
- Signed-request identity exists (TAP, Agent Pay via Web Bot Auth) but in **per-network directories** ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/secure-agentic-commerce/) ✅).
- KYA is fragmented across IETF drafts, vendor KYC-of-principal, and on-chain registries (§1.1).
- Bad actors exploit the gap. Amazon alleged Comet disguised itself as Chrome and patched around a block within 24 hours ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/03/10/judge-blocks-perplexitys-ai-bot-from-shopping-on-amazon-in-early-test-of-agentic-commerce/) ⚠️).
- Akamai: "autonomous shopping agents mimic human microbehaviors," and "agent hijacking" is used to abuse stored payment credentials ([Akamai](https://www.akamai.com/newsroom/press-release/akamai-research-commerce-becomes-the-epicenter-for-ai-bot-attacks-and-agentic-fraud-in-2026) ✅).

### 3.3 Consent and intent verification
- Verifiable Intent and AP2 mandates create signed records ✅, but **the semantic gap remains**. A signature proves *what was signed*, not that the cart matched the human's goal ([arXiv 2608.23858](https://www.emergentmind.com/papers/2608.23858) ✅).
- Identical audit logs can mean different things depending on the delegation assignment. That is the evidentiary gap a chargeback-like process would need to close (arXiv 2606.09692, as summarized by [TinyFish Current](https://current.tinyfish.ai/issue/39/market-pulse/article/32344/who-pays-to-unwind-it) ❌ not read directly).
- Users want a human checkpoint: 35% want approval before completion ([RTB House](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/consumers-like-ai-for-product-discovery-but-dont-fully-trust-it-for-purchases/) ⚠️). 40% want to stay involved in at least one stage ([Accenture](https://www.thediplomat.ro/2026/06/09/accenture-study-74-of-consumers-are-ready-to-let-an-ai-agent-shop-on-their-behalf/) ⚠️).

### 3.4 Prompt injection and manipulation of shopping/payment agents
- **AP2 red team (Jan 2026):** "Branded Whisper" (ranking manipulation) and "Vault Whisper" (data exfiltration) reliably subverted a Gemini-2.5-Flash + ADK AP2 shopping agent ([arXiv 2601.22569](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22569v1) ✅).
- **AP2 v0.2 systematic analysis:** 48 threats across 5 families and 5 architectures, 8 High (AIVSS), with 5 PoCs and a deployment-aware scanner ([arXiv 2608.23858](https://www.emergentmind.com/papers/2608.23858) ✅).
- **Microsoft Magentic Marketplace (Oct 2025):** first-proposal bias of 60–100% (a 10–30× advantage to whoever responds first). Welfare fell 65% (Sonnet-4) and 44% (GPT-5, minimal reasoning) going from 3 to 100 search results. GPT-4o, GPT-OSS-20B and Qwen3-4B were significantly manipulable. Frontier models were more resistant ([arXiv 2510.25779](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.25779v1) ✅).
- **OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications (2026 edition, released Dec 2025)** ([OWASP GenAI](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-for-2026) ✅). Goal hijacking, tool misuse and identity/privilege abuse apply directly to payment agents (`⚠ inference` mapping).
- **Task success ≠ safe execution.** Agents that completed tasks still leaked data (arXiv 2606.17114 per [TinyFish](https://current.tinyfish.ai/issue/39/market-pulse/article/32344/who-pays-to-unwind-it) ❌).

### 3.5 Hallucinated or wrong purchases
- Agents degrade with more options (Magentic, above ✅). Shopping benchmarks exist (e.g., [ShoppingBench, AAAI](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/40640) ✅; [functionality-grounded e-commerce benchmark, ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.68/) ✅), but **none measures the rate of wrong-item purchases with money at stake or the downstream dispute cost**. `⚠ inference` from benchmark scope.
- Agent-to-agent deals are "an inherently imbalanced game," and LLM quirks cause overspending or accepting unreasonable deals ([Zhu et al., arXiv 2506.00073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00073) ✅).

### 3.6 Refunds and returns for agent purchases
- Shoppers worried in-chat checkout would yield "five boxes when they actually just want it all in one" (Walmart). Per-item checkout breaks consolidated fulfillment and returns ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/what-went-wrong-with-chatgpts-instant-checkout/) ⚠️).
- Trust for agent purchases is conditional on returns. The RTB House US figures (22–42% by generation) are for ≤$250 with a 7-day return window ([Digital Transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/consumers-like-ai-for-product-discovery-but-dont-fully-trust-it-for-purchases/) ⚠️).
- UCP keeps the merchant as merchant of record and routes returns through normal merchant channels ([Eco](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14839408-universal-commerce-protocol-ucp-explained) ⚠️). **No protocol has an agent-initiated return/RMA capability that is widely deployed.** `⚠ inference`. UCP has an "Order" capability, but return flows are not documented in what I reviewed.

### 3.7 Merchant catalog readiness
- Under 1% of 500 sampled product pages are "LLM-ready" ([Mirakl via Beet.TV](https://www.beet.tv/2026/08/fewer-than-1-of-product-pages-are-llm-ready-mirakls-meadon.html) ⚠️, vendor methodology).
- Missing real-time inventory, promotions, loyalty and pickup sank Instant Checkout ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/what-went-wrong-with-chatgpts-instant-checkout/) ⚠️).
- Long-tail merchants on WooCommerce, Magento or custom stacks are invisible to UCP agents unless they build a manifest and handler ([Eco](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14839408-universal-commerce-protocol-ucp-explained) ⚠️). PayPal's Store Sync and Cymbio are aimed at this ([Dev docs](https://developer.paypal.com/store-sync/overview) ✅).

### 3.8 Agent-to-agent negotiation and settlement
- LLM bargaining research shows sequential price discrimination wins, and quick conceders (especially buyers) lose ([Bergemann et al., arXiv 2604.16472](https://econpapers.repec.org/paper/arxpapers/2604.16472.htm) ✅).
- Mastercard AP4M aims to "establish common rules" for M2M, but a partner calls it "still in their early stages" ([Mastercard](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2026/june/mastercard-launches-agent-pay-for-machines.html) ✅).
- There is no escrow or conditional-settlement standard for negotiated agent deals (`⚠ inference`). HN builders keep reinventing pieces of it: "pays once when the reply is lost" idempotency, authorization middleware, policy layers ([HN Algolia](https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search?query=agent%20payments&tags=story&numericFilters=created_at_i>1767225600) ✅).

### 3.9 Micropayments for content and APIs
- x402: about $50M cumulative and 165M transactions by late April 2026, with an average of about $0.30 per call ([Eco](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14839400-what-is-agentic-commerce-the-2026-guide) ⚠️). Activity is declining from 2025 highs, and cumulative counts range from 120M to 187M depending on tracker, which suggests non-organic inflation ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-x402-market-share-dominance/) ⚠️).
- Bazaar: median listed price $0.014 per call. 3.5% of listings have organic repeat demand. Estimated organic GMV is in the low thousands of dollars per month ([survey](https://github.com/rikocr8orh8/x402-bazaar-survey/blob/master/POST.md) ❌).
- `⚠ inference`: the bottleneck is **demand and discovery of worth-paying-for endpoints**, not rails. Fiat-funded micropayments (no crypto wallet needed) for mainstream consumers and creators are under-served.

### 3.10 Reconciliation of agent spend
- Agents pay across SPTs, virtual cards, stablecoin wallets and PayPal balances. Closed-loop tools (Ramp, Brex) reconcile only their own instruments (§2.4).
- Attribution is broken. "You can sell through it, but you can't fully measure it" ([Opascope](https://opascope.com/insights/ai-shopping-assistant-guide-2026-agentic-commerce-protocols/) ⚠️).

### 3.11 Dispute evidence
- VI's selective disclosure "allows transaction details to be verified for fraud mitigation or dispute resolution," but no network rule says what evidence wins an agent dispute ([Mastercard VI](https://www.mastercard.com/au/en/news-and-trends/stories/2026/verifiable-intent.html) ✅).
- `⚠ inference`: an **"agent dispute evidence bundle"** (mandate + pre-signing context hash + agent identity + merchant capability snapshot + delivery proof) that maps to Visa CE3.0-style compelling evidence and PayPal's own SNAD/INR rules has no owner.

### 3.12 Price discrimination against (or by) agents
- Strategic agents price-discriminate effectively in bargaining ([arXiv 2604.16472](https://econpapers.repec.org/paper/arxpapers/2604.16472.htm) ✅).
- Signed agent identity (TAP's browsing vs. purchasing tags) gives merchants a clean signal to price differently for agents ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/secure-agentic-commerce/) ✅). `⚠ inference`: no consumer-side tool audits whether an agent was shown worse prices than a human session.

### 3.13 Accessibility
- No source found that treats shopping agents as assistive technology, or that tests agent checkout for blind or low-vision users. ❌ evidence gap. `⚠ inference`: agentic checkout could be the biggest accessibility upgrade in e-commerce, and the European Accessibility Act (applies from June 2025) raises the stakes in the EU. Not re-verified this session ❌.

## 7a. Twenty emerging problems nobody owns yet

Each item: **problem** → evidence → why it's unsolved. Most are `⚠ inference` syntheses built on the cited evidence.

1. **Agent dispute taxonomy and adjudication ("authorized but wrong").** Evidence: no liability shift, and non-fraud agent disputes have no owner ([Worldpay](https://worldpay.com/insights/articles/agentic-commerce-liability-is-still-being-written) ✅). Reason codes don't fit ([Chargeflow](https://www.chargeflow.io/blog/agentic-commerce-regulation-what-merchants-need-to-know) ⚠️). Unsolved because networks move through EMVCo and FIDO on multi-year cycles and fraud vendors only guarantee fraud.
2. **Pre-signature context attestation.** Evidence: mandates don't cover A2A/MCP inputs that shape the cart ([arXiv 2608.23858](https://www.emergentmind.com/papers/2608.23858) ✅). Unsolved: needs a hash or transcript of "what the agent saw and why," and no protocol mandates one.
3. **Portable agent dispute evidence bundle.** Evidence: VI enables selective disclosure for disputes, but no rule defines winning evidence ([Mastercard](https://www.mastercard.com/au/en/news-and-trends/stories/2026/verifiable-intent.html) ✅). Unsolved: evidence formats are per-network and per-processor.
4. **Wallet-neutral agent identity and reputation registry.** Evidence: Visa and Mastercard host separate directories "to start" ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/secure-agentic-commerce/) ✅). KYA is fragmented ([BlockEden](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2026/04/26/kya-know-your-agent-new-kyc-a16z-skyfire-fatf-compliance-battleground/) ⚠️). Unsolved: every registry is owned by a rail with an incentive.
5. **Agent-initiated returns/RMA and consolidated fulfillment.** Evidence: the "five boxes" problem, and returns drive trust ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/what-went-wrong-with-chatgpts-instant-checkout/) ⚠️, [RTB House](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/consumers-like-ai-for-product-discovery-but-dont-fully-trust-it-for-purchases/) ⚠️). Unsolved: protocols stop at order confirmation in practice.
6. **Graduated autonomy / consent UX between "approve every purchase" and "fully autonomous."** Evidence: 9% would let agents pay, 35% want approval, 40% want to stay involved in one stage (§5.3). AP2 v0.2 adds Human-Not-Present ✅. Unsolved: no consumer-grade policy language ("auto-buy refills under $40 from merchants with ≥X protection").
7. **Injection-resistant shopping-agent runtime with payment-specific guardrails.** Evidence: AP2 agents subverted by simple prompts ✅. Akamai reports agent hijacking used to abuse stored credentials ✅. Unsolved: general LLM guardrails don't know payment semantics (payee changes, quantity inflation, ranking manipulation).
8. **First-mover and ranking-manipulation auditing for agent recommendations.** Evidence: 60–100% first-proposal bias ([Magentic](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.25779v1) ✅). Unsolved: no one audits whether an agent's choice was fair to the consumer.
9. **Agent price-discrimination detection.** Evidence: signed agent tags make agents identifiable ✅, and strategic price discrimination works ([arXiv 2604.16472](https://econpapers.repec.org/paper/arxpapers/2604.16472.htm) ✅). Unsolved: no consumer-side comparison of agent vs. human quotes.
10. **Cross-instrument agent spend reconciliation for SMBs and freelancers.** Evidence: Ramp/Brex reconcile only their own cards (§2.4). Agents pay via SPTs, stablecoins and wallets. Unsolved: no neutral ledger across rails.
11. **Long-tail merchant agent-readiness (non-Shopify).** Evidence: <1% of pages LLM-ready ⚠️. Non-Shopify merchants must self-build UCP ([Eco](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14839408-universal-commerce-protocol-ucp-explained) ⚠️). Unsolved: Shopify serves its own merchants and PayPal Store Sync is sales-led ✅.
12. **Real-time inventory, promotions and loyalty in agent checkout.** Evidence: the missing features that sank Instant Checkout ⚠️. Unsolved: UCP extensions exist in principle, but loyalty/coupon extensions aren't standardized.
13. **Agent-commerce attribution and analytics.** Evidence: "you can't fully measure it" ([Opascope](https://opascope.com/insights/ai-shopping-assistant-guide-2026-agentic-commerce-protocols/) ⚠️). Unsolved: no standard referral or agent-session identifiers in checkout data.
14. **Human-in-the-loop step-up that doesn't break the agent flow.** Evidence: UCP's ECP exists for 3DS and approvals ✅, and PSD2/SCA blocks agent flows ([Worldpay](https://worldpay.com/insights/articles/agentic-commerce-liability-is-still-being-written) ✅). Unsolved: no wallet-native "push to phone, approve, resume" pattern across protocols.
15. **Fiat micropayments for mainstream consumers and creators (no crypto wallet).** Evidence: x402 is crypto-first, has low organic demand, and averages about $0.30 (§3.9). Unsolved: card economics fail below about $1, and stablecoin UX excludes most consumers.
16. **Escrow and conditional settlement for agent-to-agent deals.** Evidence: AP4M is still defining common rules ✅. HN builders reinvent idempotency and credit ✅. Unsolved: no programmable, dispute-aware escrow primitive.
17. **Agent-impersonation scam defense ("I'm your PayPal assistant").** Evidence: PSD3/PSR makes PSPs refund impersonation via their channels and gives platforms liability ✅. Commerce phishing more than doubled (Akamai ✅). Unsolved: no verified-agent badge for consumer-facing chat channels.
18. **Agent-transaction flagging for AML/SAR (stablecoin and fiat).** Evidence: draft Treasury requirement to distinguish agent-initiated transactions ❌. EMVCo "Agentic Transaction Indicators" is future work ⚠️. Unsolved: no field or standard yet.
19. **Household and shared delegation (family agents, caregivers).** Evidence: UPI Circle delegates to a secondary user or agent ([Yahoo/Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/india-eyes-ai-driven-upi-084800065.html) ⚠️). Nothing equivalent exists in Western wallets (`⚠ inference`). Unsolved: Western wallets are single-principal.
20. **Accessibility-first agent checkout.** Evidence: no research found (❌ gap). Unsolved: protocols assume a sighted human for step-up and review screens.

## 7b. Over-saturated spaces to avoid (or differentiate sharply)

1. **"Chat-to-checkout" for a single merchant or assistant.** OpenAI retired its own version. Google, Microsoft, Perplexity and Amazon all ship one ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/24/openai-revamps-shopping-experience-in-chatgpt-after-instant-checkout.html) ⚠️, [Microsoft](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/january-2026/conversations-that-convert-copilot-checkout-and-brand-agents) ✅).
2. **New agent-payment protocols.** AP2, ACP, UCP, x402, MPP, TAP, VI, KYAPay, P3P and XBPP already exist, and foundations are consolidating them (§1).
3. **Crypto micropayment rails / API paywalls on x402.** Activity is down from 2025 highs, 3.5% organic demand, and the top 10 sellers take 89% ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-x402-market-share-dominance/) ⚠️, [survey](https://github.com/rikocr8orh8/x402-bazaar-survey/blob/master/POST.md) ❌).
4. **Agent wallets / virtual cards for agents.** Stripe Issuing for agents, Link agent wallet, Ramp Agent Cards, Privy, Coinbase Agentic Wallets, Crossmint, Nekuda, Natural Cards ([Stripe](https://stripe.com/au/blog/everything-we-announced-at-sessions-2026) ✅, [Ramp](https://ramp.com/blog/ai-agents-finance) ✅, [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/natural-raises-30m-series-a-to-build-payments-infrastructure-for-ai-agents-302829855.html) ✅).
5. **Generic "spending limits / policy engine for agents."** Covered by Stripe agent guardrails (preview), Ramp, Brex and several HN projects ✅.
6. **AI expense categorization and receipt bots.** Ramp, Brex, Intuit, Xero and BILL (§2.4).
7. **Basic product-feed-to-AI syndication.** Shopify Agentic Storefronts, PayPal Store Sync, Klarna APP and Google Merchant Center (§2.1).
8. **"AI shopping assistant that finds deals."** Honey-style features exist in every assistant. Alexa for Shopping already does price-triggered actions ⚠️.
