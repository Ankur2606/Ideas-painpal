# #71 Disaster Micro-Grant Distributor

> Verifies need from submitted documents and pays micro-grants instantly to PayPal/Venmo after disasters.

**Theme:** E10 Cross-border, giving & inclusion · **Group:** Giving & community · **Crowding on Devpost:** none

## Problem statement
Relief payouts are slow; but quantified payout friction data is thin.

## Proposed solution
Intake + AI document check + Payouts.

## Target users
Mutual-aid groups.

## AI contribution
Document verification, fraud screening.

## PayPal integration
Payouts; Venmo handles.
