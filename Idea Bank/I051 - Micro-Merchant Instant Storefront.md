# #51 Micro-Merchant Instant Storefront

> A WhatsApp/Instagram seller sends product photos; AI builds a catalog, prices and PayPal payment links in minutes.

**Theme:** E7 Agent-ready long tail · **Group:** Agent-ready long tail · **Crowding on Devpost:** Photo-to-listing (~2)

## Problem statement
Informal sellers trade over chat with no checkout, which pushes buyers to unprotected payments.

## Proposed solution
Photos → AI product cards → Payment Links / catalog → shareable store page.

## Target users
Social sellers, Global South micro-merchants.

## AI contribution
Vision product extraction, pricing suggestions, copy.

## PayPal integration
Payment Links API; Catalog Products; Orders.

**Primitives used** (see [[30 - Primitives Legend]]):
- `P22` Payment Links
- `P30` Catalog products / billing plans
- `A02` Classification with explanation / vision estimation
- `A12` Eligibility reasoning and messaging

## Competitors and prior art
Shopify, Take App, Linktree commerce

Instant storefront builders are common.

**Verification status:** ⚠️ desk research only. The competitor names above come from the landscape and Devpost scans, not from a per-idea novelty hunt. Treat "no competitor found" as unproven.

## Differentiation
Low-moderate.

## Feasibility
High.

## MVP scope
5 photos → store → payment.
