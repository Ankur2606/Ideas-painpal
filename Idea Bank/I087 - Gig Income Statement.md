# #87 Gig Income Statement

> Turns a freelancer's PayPal payouts and invoices into a verifiable income statement for renting or loans.

**Theme:** E10 Cross-border, giving & inclusion · **Group:** Inclusive money · **Crowding on Devpost:** none

## Problem statement
Gig workers struggle to prove income.

## Proposed solution
Transaction Search aggregation + AI narrative + signed PDF.

## Target users
Gig workers.

## AI contribution
Categorisation, narrative.

## PayPal integration
Transaction Search; Log in with PayPal.
