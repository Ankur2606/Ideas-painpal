# #85 Plain-Language Payment Consent

> Renders any PayPal order, subscription or Pay Later offer in the user's language and reading level before approval.

**Theme:** E10 Cross-border, giving & inclusion · **Group:** Inclusive money · **Crowding on Devpost:** none
